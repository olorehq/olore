<h2 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="plans-that-include-claude-tag" aria-labelledby="_R_35deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#plans-that-include-claude-tag" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Plans that include Claude Tag">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_35deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Plans that include Claude Tag</span></h2>
<span data-as="p">Claude Tag is available on Team and Enterprise plans, on Anthropic’s first-party service. It isn’t available on individual plans (Free, Pro, Max, or Ultra), or for third-party deployments. To use it, your organization pairs its Slack workspace with its Claude organization; see <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/setup-overview" class="link">the setup overview</a> for the full prerequisites.</span>
<span data-as="p">If you’re choosing between Claude products for Slack-shaped work, <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works#how-claude-tag-differs-from-cowork-and-claude-code" class="link">how Claude Tag differs from Cowork and Claude Code</a> compares them directly: team work in shared channels is Claude Tag; personal work on your own files is Cowork or Claude Code.</span>
<h3 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="who-needs-a-claude-seat" aria-labelledby="_R_95deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#who-needs-a-claude-seat" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Who needs a Claude seat">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_95deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Who needs a Claude seat</span></h3>
<span data-as="p">Slack users don’t each need a Claude seat to work with Claude in channels.</span>
<ul>
<li><strong>In channels</strong>: by default, anyone in the paired Slack workspace can tag <code>@Claude</code> in a channel, and an Owner can <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#restrict-who-can-use-claude" class="link">restrict who can use Claude</a> to people in your Claude organization or, on Enterprise, to specific roles. Channel work bills by usage to your organization’s usage balance, under a <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/set-spend-limit" class="link">spend limit</a> an Owner sets.</li>
<li><strong>In group DMs</strong>: the same restriction on who can use Claude applies, and the work bills to your organization’s usage balance. See <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#group-dms" class="link">Group DMs</a>.</li>
<li><strong>In one-to-one DMs</strong>: a DM from a member who has connected a Claude account runs on that account and bills to that person’s seat. For the DM to work, that person needs either a seat that includes Claude Code or, on the Enterprise plan, Cowork access on a <strong>Standard</strong> seat or a usage-based <strong>Chat</strong> seat. A <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#direct-messages-from-members-without-a-claude-account" class="link">DM from a member who hasn’t connected a Claude account</a> can bill to your organization for a limited time.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="where-claude-tag-runs" aria-labelledby="_R_f5deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#where-claude-tag-runs" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Where Claude Tag runs">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_f5deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Where Claude Tag runs</span></h2>
<span data-as="p">Claude Tag works in Slack. You interact with it by writing in a Slack channel, thread, or direct message, and it replies there. Mention <code>@Claude</code> in a channel to guarantee it picks the message up.</span>
<span data-as="p">When Claude works on a task, it runs in an ephemeral sandbox, not on your computer. The sandbox is created when a conversation starts, holds any code or files Claude is working with, and is discarded when the conversation goes idle. See <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works" class="link">how Claude Tag works</a> for the full lifecycle.</span>
<span data-as="p">You extend what Claude can reach, like your repositories, ticketing systems, data warehouses, and custom tools, through <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/add-connections" class="link">Claude Tag connectors</a>, <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/customize" class="link">plugins, and skills</a>. An Owner or a <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#delegate-claude-tag-administration" class="link">Claude Tag admin</a> configures these for all of Slack, for one workspace, or for one channel. Members’ own claude.ai connectors are separate from that configuration; Claude can use them in a channel for the member’s own requests, as <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/personal-connectors" class="link">personal connectors in channels</a> describes.</span>

<h2 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="billing-and-spend-limits" aria-labelledby="_R_p5deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#billing-and-spend-limits" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Billing and spend limits">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_p5deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Billing and spend limits</span></h2>
<span data-as="p">Adding Claude to Slack doesn’t add a per-seat charge. Channel, thread, and group DM work is billed by usage instead: it draws from a <strong>usage balance</strong>, an amount in your organization’s billing currency that an Owner funds. A <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/set-spend-limit" class="link">spend limit</a> caps how much of that balance Claude Tag can use each billing period.</span>
<span data-as="p">One-to-one direct messages from members who have connected a Claude account don’t draw from this balance. Such a DM runs on the sender’s own claude.ai account and follows that seat’s usual usage limits, so the organization spend limit doesn’t apply to it. A <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#direct-messages-from-members-without-a-claude-account" class="link">DM from a member who hasn’t connected a Claude account</a> can draw from this balance.</span>
<span data-as="p">To learn what your team’s usage costs, run a pilot with a spend limit set and watch the per-channel breakdown on the <a href="https://claude.ai/admin-settings/usage/claude-tag" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer" class="link">usage page in your admin settings</a>. <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/set-spend-limit" class="link">Set a spend limit</a> covers how to fund the balance on each plan, set the limit, and what happens when usage reaches it.</span>
<div class="tm-eyebrow"><span class="tm-swatch tm-swatch-users"></span>For end users</div>
<h2 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="put-claude-tag-to-work" aria-labelledby="_R_135deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#put-claude-tag-to-work" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Put Claude Tag to work">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_135deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Put Claude Tag to work</span></h2>
<span data-as="p">If Claude Tag is in your channel, you can use it now. (If it isn’t there yet, an Owner in your Claude organization runs setup: see <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/setup-overview" class="link">Set up Claude Tag</a>.) Anyone in the channel can hand it work, and channel work bills to the organization, not to you.</span>
<span data-as="p">What it can reach starts with the channel you’re in. The fastest way to find out is to ask it: <code>@Claude what can you access from this channel?</code> Or, if you’re signed in to your Claude organization, click <strong>Configure</strong> in the footer of a Claude reply in the channel to see its <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/glossary#connection" class="link">connections</a>, the external services an admin has connected for that channel. Replies in org-shared channels have no Configure link.</span>
<span data-as="p">For your own requests, Claude can also <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/personal-connectors" class="link">use the connectors on your claude.ai account</a>, after you allow it.</span>
<span data-as="p">In a one-to-one DM, Claude runs on your own claude.ai account instead of a channel’s setup. An Owner can turn off DMs for the whole organization; see <a href="/docs/claude-tag/admins/restrict-access#allow-or-disable-direct-messages" class="link">Allow or disable direct messages</a>.</span>
<h3 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="common-uses" aria-labelledby="_R_1d5deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#common-uses" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Common uses">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_1d5deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Common uses</span></h3>
<span data-as="p">Each link opens a guide with the prompts to paste and the connections the task needs.</span>
<ul>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/watch-monitors" class="link">Watch monitors and alerts</a>: scheduled dashboard checks, and alerts investigated as they arrive. Needs a monitoring connection like Datadog, Sentry, or PagerDuty.</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/triage-requests" class="link">Triage requests</a>: an intake channel where Claude answers what it can, flags duplicates, and routes the rest. Works on Slack content alone.</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/find-answers" class="link">Find answers in your docs</a>: policy and runbook questions answered with the source. Needs a docs connection like Google Drive, Notion, or Confluence.</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/answer-data-questions" class="link">Answer data questions</a>: a plain-language question becomes a warehouse query and a chart. Needs a data warehouse connection.</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/track-projects" class="link">Track projects and chase approvals</a>: standing status digests and follow-ups that run until an approval lands</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/create-artifacts" class="link">Turn threads into docs and tickets</a>: a settled discussion becomes the decision doc, the customer reply, or the filed tickets</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/fix-bugs" class="link">Fix bugs</a>: a bug reported in the channel comes back as a draft pull request. Needs GitHub.</li>
<li><a href="/docs/claude-tag/users/use-cases/your-own-channel" class="link">Work from your own channel</a>: scratch questions, digests of channels you don’t follow, and follow-ups on what you said you’d do</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="flex whitespace-pre-wrap group font-semibold" id="set-claude-tag-up-once-for-everyone" aria-labelledby="_R_1n5deehkd74t9_"><div class="absolute" tabindex="-1"><a href="#set-claude-tag-up-once-for-everyone" class="-ml-10 flex items-center opacity-0 border-0 group-hover:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100 focus:outline-0 group/link" aria-label="Navigate to header: Set Claude Tag up once for everyone">​<div class="size-6 rounded-md flex items-center justify-center shadow-xs text-gray-400 dark:text-white/50 dark:bg-background-dark dark:brightness-[1.35] dark:ring-1 dark:hover:brightness-150 bg-white ring-1 ring-gray-400/30 dark:ring-gray-700/25 hover:ring-gray-400/60 dark:hover:ring-white/20 group-focus/link:border-2 group-focus/link:border-primary dark:group-focus/link:border-primary-light"><svg aria-hidden="true" width="18" height="18" stroke-width="2" class="size-3 shrink-0"><use href="#mi-mint-1-Link"></use></svg></div></a></div><span id="_R_1n5deehkd74t9_" class="cursor-pointer">Set Claude Tag up once for everyone</span></h2>
<span data-as="p">You set up Claude Tag once, at <a href="https://claude.ai/admin-settings/claude-tag" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer" class="link"><code>claude.ai/admin-settings/claude-tag</code></a>, and you must be an Owner in your Claude organization to do it. The setup page at that URL walks you through it:</span>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pair your Slack workspace</strong>: send <code>@Claude connect</code> in Slack to get a pairing code, then enter it on the setup page.</li>
<li><strong>Set a monthly spend limit, add Claude to channels, and launch</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<span data-as="p">Once you launch, everyone in a channel Claude is in can use Claude Tag immediately, with no per-user setup.</span>
<span data-as="p">Claude Tag starts with no access of its own to your external systems. After you launch, you connect the services Claude will work in, such as your issue tracker or data warehouse, and grant repositories to the Claude GitHub App. Members can also let Claude use their own <a href="/docs/claude-tag/concepts/personal-connectors" class="link">personal connectors</a> for their requests.</span>
