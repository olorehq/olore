\# Vibe

Vibe sets the emotional quality of the image. It shapes mood and atmosphere so the output reflects your intended tone.

Choose descriptors that clearly express the feeling you want to convey. Terms like serene, dramatic, playful, or tense help the model establish a consistent emotional direction.
