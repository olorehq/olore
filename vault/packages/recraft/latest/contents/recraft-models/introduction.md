# Introduction

> Recraft has more than one model. Recraft's own models improve generation over generation.

## Recraft Text To Image Models

Recraft has released four generations of its own model:

> [card] [V4.1](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1.md)
>
> This is the latest generation. It delivers photorealism that actually looks real, sharper vector and illustration work, and strong results even from short prompts. It’s also the first generation to introduce Utility variants, built for flat, predictable outcomes, making it perfect for product shots or mockups. It's also the first to introduce Flash for when you need fast generations.

> [card] [V4](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-V4.md)
>
> This model is the previous generation and still a dependable default for everyday raster and vector generation. It's also the more opinionated of the two, leaning further into stylization than V4.1, with a knack for super stylish, unique photoshoots.

> [card] [V3](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-V3.md)
>
> The V3 model costs less per image than V4 and generates faster too. It's built for style consistency, precise text positioning, and artistic level control, and it's home to some of Recraft's most-loved styles, including Risograph and Hard flash. The vector variant is a strong pick for illustration work.

> [card] [V2](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-V2.md)
>
> This is Recraft's most affordable model. It's the one to reach for a specific look, like the long-loved Plastic 3D style, or when you need vector icons with consistent line width and shape across a whole set.

## Other Models

> [card] [V4 Styles](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-styles.md)
>
> Give it one reference image or up to 10, and V4 Styles holds the whole composition — texture, palette, and rendering — not just the general vibe, with no training required. In a blind, third-party-judged benchmark against GPT Image 2 High, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro, Flux 2 Max, and others, Precise mode won 91.6% of its head-to-head matchups. Ships as four models — Recraft V4 Styles, V4 Styles Pro, V4 Styles Vector, and V4 Styles Pro Vector — available now in Recraft Studio and via the API.

## Variants within a generation

Most generations ship in more than one variant:

* **Standard** is the default raster model.
* **Pro** outputs at higher resolution, for print-ready assets and large-format work.
* **Vector** generates editable SVG files directly, instead of raster images.
* **Utility** (V4.1 only) uses flat lighting and front-facing composition, built for mockups and product shots rather than creative exploration.
* **Flash** (V4.1 only) for faster generations.
