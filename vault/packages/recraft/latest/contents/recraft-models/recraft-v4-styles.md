# Recraft V4 Styles

## Style it once, every image matches

Every generator claims to match a style. Most of them just “approximate” it. V4 Styles, released in August 2026, holds the whole look: texture, composition, and rendering, not just the general vibe.

Give it one reference image or as many as 10. Similar references sharpen the match. A wider mix widens the range the model can work in. Either way, nothing needs training or fine-tuning. Attach a reference, generate, and you’re done.

And unlike models that only recognize styles born inside them, V4 Styles reads references from anywhere: other generators, brand libraries, or an asset pulled from a client's old campaign. If it exists as an image, V4 Styles can learn from it.

## Availability

Recraft V4 Styles and V4 Styles Pro are available now in Recraft Studio and through the API. To use V4 Styles in Recraft Studio, select the combined Recraft V4.1 model, and Recraft V4.1 and V4 Styles will be both included.

## The benchmark

In a blind, third-party-judged benchmark across 159 real-world styles, Precise mode won 91.6% of its head-to-head matchups.

The comparison set: GPT Image 2 High, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro, Flux 2 Max, Nano Banana 2, Midjourney, and Krea 2 Large, each at the version available on August 14, 2026. Recraft designed the test, but five independent evaluators per matchup, sourced through a third-party crowdsourcing platform and blind to which model made which image, decided every result. Full methodology is available on request.

A note on the field: these are strong models, and several of them are the reason “style match” became table stakes in the first place. This benchmark measures one specific thing, holding a style across a generation, not overall image quality. It's the thing V4 Styles was built to win on.

The named competitor models identify the visual styles used as benchmark inputs. None of the companies behind them participated in, reviewed, or endorsed this benchmark, and none is affiliated with Recraft.

## Precise or flexible, your call

**Precise** is the one to reach for when the brief says “match this exactly.” It follows a reference's texture, palette, and rendering with the kind of loyalty that holds up across a full batch, not just a lucky first image.

**Flexible** is the default setting and the looser read, closer to the style-matching Recraft V3 users already know, refined for V4. Use it when the goal is the mood of a reference, not a copy of it.

## Four new models, one job

V4 Styles ships as four models: **Recraft V4 Styles, Recraft V4 Styles Pro, Recraft V4 Styles Vector,** and **Recraft V4 Styles Pro Vector** (`recraftv4_styles`, `recraftv4_styles_pro`, `recraftv4_styles_vector`, `recraftv4_styles_pro_vector`). V4 Styles is the go-to for routine work, and V4 Styles Pro is the one to pick when you need more high resolution work. And the Vector models are specifically made for vector work.

Every other model in the V4 family also supports style-consistent generation now, at the same price as generating without a style.
