## ⛔ What NOT to Do

**❌ Don't write essays** — 1–2 sentences max. Longer prompts confuse the model.

**❌ Don't stack actions** — "Person walks, turns, picks up cup, sits" = bad. One action per prompt.

**❌ Don't describe what's already visible** — The model sees your image. Describe what should *change*.

**❌ Don't contradict yourself** — "Slow calm + explosive motion" = the model doesn't know what to pick.

**❌ Don't be vague** — "Make it cinematic" = nothing. "Slow dolly-in, shallow depth of field" = works.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Simpler = better.** 1–3 sentences max.
2. **One prompt = one action.** Generate multiple clips instead of stacking.
3. **Describe the change, not the scene.** The model already sees your image.
4. **Modifiers go at the end**, not the beginning.
5. **Combine categories:** camera + atmosphere + modifier = strongest results.
6. **Start subtle, then scale up.** Level 2 first, push to 4 if needed.
