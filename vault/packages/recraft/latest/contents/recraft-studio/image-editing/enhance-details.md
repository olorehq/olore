# Enhance

Enhance regenerates the image you picked at higher quality, keeping your composition and scene. Small details get redrawn rather than sharpened, so things like hands, faces, text, and fine textures often come out more detailed than in the original. You get that same image enhanced, not a new interpretation of the prompt.

It pairs naturally with the Recraft V4.1 Flash model: explore directions quickly, then take the one that works to Pro-level quality without losing what you chose. It also works on any generation, not only Flash results.

**Choose a model**

**Recraft V4.1 Pro** regenerates your image with our highest-quality model, bringing it up to that model's level of detail at 2048×2048. 10 credits.

Two enhancement levels:

* **Subtle** stays close to the original. It cleans up noise and raises contrast while preserving the letterforms, textures, and small nuances of the image you generated.
* **Moderate**, the default, rebuilds small details more actively. It is the stronger option in most cases, improving things like anatomy, line consistency, and text readability, while keeping the layout and the main elements intact.

**Recraft Creative Upscale** enhances and enlarges in one step, up to 16MP. Choose it when you need a larger image as well as a better one. 20 credits.

Two target sizes: **to 4MP** and **to 16MP**.

**What to expect**

Enhancement improves quality and often corrects small errors from the original generation, though not every issue can be resolved and results vary by image. Photography and product scenes hold up more consistently than layouts with typography.

**Enhance or upscale**

Crisp upscale enlarges an image without changing its content, which is what you want for icons, vector-style illustrations, and anything where consistency matters. Enhance regenerates the image, so reach for it when you want the result itself to be better, not just bigger.
