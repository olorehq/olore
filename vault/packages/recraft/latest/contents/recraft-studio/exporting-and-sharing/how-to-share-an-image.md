# How to share an image

You can share an image in multiple ways:

* right-clicking on the in the canvas and selecting **Copy image link**
* opening the image in the Community, Favorites, or History panels and clicking on the **link icon** to copy the URL.

You can then share the URL. A Recraft account is required to view shared images.

If the image is public, the link will be viewable by others. On paid plans, you can also set privacy settings before sharing.

For **private images** (images generated with a subscription), this link will not open for other users. To share a private image together with its generation settings, you need to create a **project** and add the image there. This allows you to share both the image and its full generation context in one place.
