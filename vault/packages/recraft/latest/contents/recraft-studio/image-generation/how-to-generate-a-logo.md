# How to generate a logo

There are two ways to make a logo in Recraft:

* **Recraft Logo Maker** guides you from your business idea to a finished logo. Use it if you’d rather answer questions than write prompts.
* **The Recraft Studio canvas** gives you full control with prompts, models, and styles. Use it if you already know what you want or want to fine-tune every detail.

### Recraft Logo Maker

Logo Maker works on desktop and in your phone’s browser. It isn’t available in the Recraft mobile app yet.

#### How to open it

Go to recraft.ai/logo, or open it from:

* The **Logo** tab in the left sidebar on the home page
* The link to Logo Maker on the Recraft Studio canvas
* The [AI logo generator](https://www.recraft.ai/generate/logos) page

#### How it works

1. **Tell us about your business.** Enter your brand name and a short description, then choose the type of logo you want and how it should feel.

2. **Pick a style.** Choose from 24 logo styles picked by Recraft designers, or upload your own images as references.

3. **Review four directions.** Logo Maker creates four different logos, choosing the best model for each one.

4. **Refine your favorite.** Adjust its colors, edit it, or create variations. You can also generate four new directions or switch to another style.

5. **See it on real products.** Preview your logo in mockups on dozens of products. Mockups appear instantly and don’t use credits.

6. **Download your logo.** Get your final logo as a vector file (SVG).

#### Credits

Logo Maker uses credits for generating and editing logos. The total depends on how many rounds and edits you make.

To learn what you can do with your logo, see [Commercial rights and ownership](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/plans-and-billing/commercial-rights-and-ownership.md).

### Generate a logo on the Recraft Studio canvas

1. **Create a new image.** Open your project and add a new image frame on the canvas.
2. **Select a vector style.** For the best results, choose one of Recraft’s vector models and styles designed for logo creation, such as Geometric Logo, Sharp Drawn Logo, or Playful Typographic. You can find more by typing “logo” in the style search bar.
3. **Write your prompt.** Describe the logo you want to create, for example: “logo ancient Greek sculpture, clever negative space”. To include text in the logo, place it in quotation marks. For instance, typing “Broccoli” will add that word as stylized text.
4. **Click the generate button** to create your logo.

**Need more than a logo?** Use [Design Agent](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/design-agent.md) to build a full brand kit.
