# Curated styles

To apply a curated style, open the Style library from the prompt panel before generating your image, and go to the **Explore** tab. It includes the new V4 Styles library alongside the existing searchable feed of AI-generated styles and styles selected by the Recraft team. If you frequently use a particular style, click the heart icon on its thumbnail to save it — it'll appear under **Liked** in **My styles** for easy access in future projects.
