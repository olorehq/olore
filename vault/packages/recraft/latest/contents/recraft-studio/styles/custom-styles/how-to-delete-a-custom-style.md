# How to delete a custom style

1. From the canvas, create a new image or click an existing one.
2. Click the **Style button** in the prompt panel, then click the **My styles** tab on the top menu tab.
3. Click on the style you want to remove.
4. In the right panel, click on the three dots at the top right, then click **Delete style**.

Note: Style deletion is only available on the desktop version of Recraft Studio.
