# Overview

Styles define the visual characteristics of your generated images. You can choose from a wide range of curated styles categorized by type, such as photorealism, illustration, vector icon, or logo, or create your own using reference images.

## The Styles panel

The Styles panel includes several tabs and filters to help you browse and manage styles.

There are two separate places to work with styles:

* **My Styles** — a standalone library of your created and saved styles only, opened from the menu outside the canvas or the canvas dropdown menu. No Explore section here.
* **Style library** — opened via the Style button in the prompt panel, with two tabs: **Explore** (all available styles) and **My styles** (your recent, liked, and created styles).

When applying a style, you can choose how closely the output should follow it:

* **Precise** — follows the style's reference material closely, for when matching the exact look matters most.
* **Flexible** — matches the general vibe while giving the model more liberty, for when mood matters more than an exact match.

To guide a generation with reference images, attach them directly from the main prompt panel rather than through a separate step — see [How to create a custom style](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-studio/styles/custom-styles/how-to-create-a-custom-style.md) for the full flow.

## The Styles panel has two tabs:

* **Explore**: Browse curated and AI-generated styles. Filter by category (All, Photo, Illustration, Graphic design, Icon, Logo), or use the Type and Model dropdowns to narrow results further. Search by descriptive terms like *"comics"*, *"3D"*, or *"line art"* — Recraft surfaces visually similar styles even when the style names don't match. Click **Create style** here to build a custom style from reference images.
* **My styles**: Styles you've saved, grouped into:
  * **Created by me**: Styles you've built yourself.
  * **Liked**: Styles you've saved from Explore for reuse, indicated by the heart icon on a style's thumbnail.
