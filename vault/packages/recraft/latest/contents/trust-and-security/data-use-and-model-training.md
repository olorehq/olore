# Data use and model training

To give you the most reliable, relevant, and high-quality results, Recraft continuously improves its AI models. One way we do this is by using content from our users, including images you create or upload and text you enter in prompts or chat mode.

API inputs and outputs are never used for model training. Data sent through the Recraft API is processed only to generate requested results and is not stored or used to improve models.

Both free and paid accounts are included by default, and Teams are not included by default.

If you prefer not to have your content used for model training, you can opt out at any time in your account settings. Opting out does not affect your ability to use Recraft or any of its features.

For full details, see our [Terms of Service](https://www.recraft.ai/terms).

## How to opt out

1. Log in to Recraft on desktop.
2. Go to your [Profile settings](https://www.recraft.ai/profile/settings).
3. Near **Help improve Recraft**, turn the toggle off.
4. Changes save automatically.

Once opted out, none of your future images or text inputs will be used to train our models.

## FAQ

**Is Training Opt-Out available for all users?**\
Training opt-out is not available on the Free plan. On the Pro plan, you can choose to opt out in your Profile settings by turning off the “Help improve Recraft” switch. For Team plans, the training opt-out is applied by default.

**Will opting out change how my images generate?**\
No. Opting out only changes how Recraft uses your data for model improvements. Overall, the generation quality and features remain the same.

**What data is used for training?**\
Images, prompts, and chat content may be included. Personal information such as your email, billing details, or account data is never used.

**If I delete my account, is my data kept?**\
No. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed from Recraft’s systems.

**Can I change my preference later?**\
Yes. You can return to **Profile → Preferences** at any time to re-enable or disable training use.
