The settings section is where you will add your Users and manage their access level permissions for your [ZeptoMail](https://zeptomail.zoho.com/) account. A total of 30 users can be added to your ZeptoMail account. You can click on **Settings** on the left menu panel to view **Settings** section.

![Settings ](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/settings-new.png)

## Manage Users

All the users for your ZeptoMail account can be viewed here. For each user, their role and their associated Agents can be viewed. You can filter users based on Agents or roles using the filter drop-down located below Manage Users header title.

There are 3 roles in your ZeptoMail account:

- [Postmaster](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/manage-users.html#postmaster) \- admin
- [Engineer](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/manage-users.html#engineer) \- Some privileges as listed below
- [Viewer](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/manage-users.html#viewer) \- only to view

Refer [this page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/manage-users.html) to know more about the access privileges and steps to add and remove users to your account.

Note:

- All user roles are assigned at the Account level. ZeptoMail does not allow assigning Agent-specific user roles.
- Only Postmaster Access level can buy credits. Engineer and Viewer roles can only view the credit balance.

## Activity logs

Activity logs allows you to view the activities performed by different users in your ZeptoMail account.

You can filter and view the contents based on the search criteria provided. Refer the [help page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/activity-logs.html) for more information on this.

## Notification Settings

Notification settings can be used to set credit purchase reminder emails to your registered email id. By default the notification emails are triggered based on certain criteria. You can set to receive custom notifications too. Navigate to the Notification settings section to manage your notifications. This [page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/notification-settings.html) will give you step by step information on how you can set to receive email notifications.

## Content Setting

You can save the contents of the emails that you send by enabling the corresponding option in the **Content Setting** tab.This saved content can be viewed in the Processed emails section under the particular Agent for which you enable it. These contents will be available for you to view for a period of 60 days.

## Migration

The migration section lists the logs and statuses of the data migrated from [Postmark](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/postmark-migration.html) and [SendGrid.](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/sendgrid-migration.html)

## Export Logs

In this section, you can export the last 60 days' logs and download them to your local servers.

Fill in the filter criteria and initiate export. You will be able to access the zip files, 3 days from the date of export completion. You can find the detailed procedure to export your logs [here](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/export-logs.html).

## Bounce forwarding

Bounce forwarding allows you to forward notifications of hard bounced emails to a verified email address. Detailed procedure on bounce forwarding is available [here.](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/bounce-forwarding.html "Help page")

## IP restriction

You can use the IP restriction option to include a list of IPs that will be used to send emails from your ZeptoMail account. Adding your IPs to this list will provide an additional layer of security in case your Send mail token is compromised and to prevent unauthorised parties from sending out emails.

The steps to add restricted IPs will be given in this [page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/ip-restriction.html).

## Sending limits

This section allows you to set a limit for the number of emails each Agent can send. Refer [this page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/email-limits.html) for more information on setting email limits.

## Delete Account

You can initiate the deletion process for your ZeptoMail account here. Once initiated, all Agents, domains, and the associated data will be deleted permanently. Only a user with Postmaster access can delete the account. Once you confirm deletion:

1. Email sending will be suspended
2. You will not be able to view any email logs, added domains, or reports

You can revert the deletion process within 48 hours after initiation. You can do this by accessing the UI or the link provided in the deletion notification email​

You can find the detailed information on how to delete your account [here](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/delete-account.html).

Note:

- Before you get started it is important to know that ZeptoMail is for sending transactional emails like welcome emailers, password resets emails, OTPs. We do not support sending of bulk emails or promotional emails like newsletters or marketing campaign emails. If you are looking for a bulk email provider, you can visit [Zoho Campaigns](https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/).
