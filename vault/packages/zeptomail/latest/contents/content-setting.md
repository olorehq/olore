# Content retention

You can save the contents of your emails you send in the **Content retention** section in the **Settings** menu. Once enabled, email content for all Agents will be saved for up to 60 days. You can view saved emails in the Processed Emails section within each Agent.

### Points to remember

- You can customize how long content is retained, or exclude specific Agents from this setting entirely.
- You can extend the email retention period beyond 60 days by contacting support@zeptomail.com.

Note

- Only the Postmaster can customize the duration and exempt Agents from content retention.

**To save the contents of the emails :**

1. Go to the **Settings** menu from the left panel.
2. Navigate to the **Content retention** tab.
3. Choose the **Save the email content** checkbox to save your email contents. ​
4. The contents of the emails will be saved only after enabling this setting.
5. Click **Add Customization** if you want to change the retention period or exclude specific Agents
1. To save content for a custom duration, select **Save** from the dropdown and enter the time period for that Agent.
2. To exclude an Agent from saving content, select **Do not save.**
3. You can delete or change the saved options at any time.
6. Click **Save Changes** when done.

Note:

Before you get started it is important to know that ZeptoMail is for sending transactional emails like welcome emailers, password resets emails, OTPs. We do not support sending of bulk emails or promotional emails like newsletters or marketing campaign emails. If you are looking for a bulk email provider, you can visit [Zoho Campaigns](https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/).
