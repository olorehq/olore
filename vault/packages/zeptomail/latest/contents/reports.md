![Transactional email delivery system reports by date range](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/reports-1.jpg)

### Overall Report

For a selected date range, this report provides an overview of the below-mentioned data for your account:

- **Sent** \- Total number of emails processed. If your email has 1 recipient in 'to' field, 1 recipient in 'cc' field, and 1 recipient in 'bcc' field; then the total count of emails sent is 3.
- **Delivered**\- Number of emails that have been delivered to the recipient's inbox.
- **Soft bounces** \- Number of emails that did not deliver due to temporary reasons like recipient server being down or recipient's inbox being full.
- **Hard bounces** \- Number of emails that did not deliver due to permanent reasons like invalid recipient address.
- **Click** \- Total number of clicks for your emails. These clicks are for the links embedded in your email html body.
- **Open** \- Number of times recipient opened your emails.

[Email Tracking](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/email-tracking.html) has to be enabled for getting the click and open data.

![](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/reportsoverview.png)

You can view the data either as a line or a bar graph format. Click on the graph icon drop-down on the top right corner to switch between line and bar graph.

![Select Transactional emails data format](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/rep-3.jpg)

### Browsers

![](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/reportsbrowsers.png)

## Custom Reports

In this section, you can compare the performance between your [Agents](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/agents.html). Follow the steps below to do a comparison:

1. From the _left panel_ click on **Reports**. This will open the **Reports** section.
2. Navigate to the **Custom Reports** tab. You can view the [**Compare Reports**](https://zeptomail.zoho.com/#reports/customize) section.
3. Click on the **Select Agent** drop-down and select the Agents you want to compare.
4. Choose the metric you want to compare between these Agents. You can choose any one metric from **Sent**, **Soft bounces**, **Hard bounces**, **Click** and **Open** by clicking on the associated radio button.
5. Once you have completed choosing the Agents and the metrics to be compared, the report will be generated.

![](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/customreports.png)

6. You can view the comparison report as a line graph, a bar graph or in a table format. Click on the graph icon drop-down on the top-right and select between line and bar graph.
7. You can also generate this comparison report for 3 intervals - Last 7 days, Last 15 days and Last 30 days.

### Save your Report

There is no need to generate **Compare Reports** every time. You can save your **Compare Reports**. All the latest data will be updated automatically in your saved reports. To save a report:

1. Follow the steps from 1 till 7 as above.
2. Click on **Save Report** on the top right corner of the Compare Report section.

![](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/savereport.png)

3. Give a name to your Report and click on Save. All your saved reports will be listed in this section.
4. Next time when you visit Compare Report section, scroll down and click on your saved report. This will display your saved report with the latest data.

![Saved comparison reports in Transactional email system](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/rep-saved.jpg)

5. You can delete your saved reports by clicking on the delete icon on that report. Once your saved report is deleted, you can not retrieve the data.

![You can delete your mail agents comparison report](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/rep-saved-del.jpg)

### Bounce Reports

This report gives the different types of bounces that have occurred in your account, for the desired date range. The bounce report categories the bounce data into following categories:

- Connection issues.
- Policy failures.
- Spam.
- User not found.
- Invalid DNS.
- Others.

This [page](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/bounce-error.html) gives you the detailed information on the bounce reports.

Note:

Before you get started it is important to know that ZeptoMail is for sending transactional emails like welcome emailers, password resets emails, OTPs. We do not support sending of bulk emails or promotional emails like newsletters or marketing campaign emails. If you are looking for a bulk email provider, check out [Zoho Campaigns](https://www.zoho.com/campaigns/).
