# Export Logs

You can export different logs available in your ZeptoMail account using the **Export Logs** option. The last **60 days'** logs can be exported. The exported data will be available as a zip file for 3 days from the date of completion. A maximum of _**5000 records**_ can be exported at once.​

You will be able export three types of data :

- [Email logs](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/export-logs.html#email-logs "Section navigation") \- Data related to your email like bounce, clicks and sender/recipient information.
- [Activity logs](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/export-logs.html#activity-logs "Section navigation") \- Information related to activities of different users which is listed in activity logs.
- [Suppression list logs](https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/export-logs.html#suppression-list "Section navigation") \- Data available in suppression list.

## Steps to export your email logs

01. Login to your ZeptoMail account.
02. Navigate to **Export Logs** in the Settings menu.​
03. In the **Email logs** tab, click **Initiate export.**
04. Choose whether to export the logs for a specific Agent or all Agents. You can also choose to include or exclude the email content.
05. Choose the date range. You can either choose the last 7, 15, 30, 60 days' logs or pick a custom time range.
06. You even have the option to password protect your exported data. Enter your desired password.



    ![Email logs](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/email-logs-1.png)

    ​
07. You can also choose to export logs based on the available filter options:
    01. To
    02. From
    03. Request Id
    04. Subject
    05. Client reference
    06. CC
    07. BCC
    08. Soft bounces
    09. Hard bounces
    10. Process failed
    11. Delivered
    12. Bounce category

        ![export Email logs](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/email-logs-2.png)
08. Pick the desired category and add the relevant values.
09. You can even add multiple conditions to narrow down your search. You can do this using the plus icon next to the conditions textbox.
10. Click **Initiate export.**
11. The .zip file can be downloaded from the **Dashboard** or the **Export logs** section. It will be available for a period of two days.

## Steps to export activity logs

You can export the data related to the activities of all the users of your account.

1. Login to your ZeptoMail account.
2. Go to **Export** in the **Settings** menu.
3. In the **Activity logs** tab, click **Initiate export.**
4. Choose the date range.You can either choose the last 7, 15, 30 days' logs or pick a custom time range.
5. You even have the option to password protect your exported data. Enter your desired password.
6. Select the email address whose activity you wish to export.

![Export activity log](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/activity-log-1.png)
7. Choose the criteria based on which you wish to export the files.
01. Bounce address
02. Domains
03. Email tracking
04. Files
05. Agent
06. Email template
07. Tags
08. User management
09. Webhooks
10. API token
11. Account Setting
12. Logs Export
13. Custom Report
14. Suppression List
15. Sender address
16. Export
17. Integration
18. IP restriction
19. Dedicated IP
8. Click **Initiate export.**
9. A .zip file will be available for download in **Dashboard** as well as the **Export logs** section. It will be available for a period of two days.

## Steps to export suppression list

1. Login to your ZeptoMail account.
2. Go to **Export** in the **Settings** menu.
3. In the **Suppression list** tab, click **Initiate export.**
4. Choose the date range. You can either choose the last 7, 15, 30 days' logs or pick a custom time range.
5. You even have the option to password protect your exported data. Enter your desired password.

![Export supression list](https://www.zohowebstatic.com/sites/zweb/images/zeptomail/supression-list-1.png)
6. Choose the type of suppression based on which you wish to export the emails. Click **Initiate export.**
7. A .zip file will be available for download in **Dashboard** as well as the **Export logs** section. It will be available for a period of two days.
