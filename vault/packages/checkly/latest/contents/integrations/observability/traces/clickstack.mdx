Self-hosted ClickStack and Managed ClickStack in ClickHouse Cloud end the same way. Checkly sends the spans to your collector on port 4318 with an `authorization` header.

1. Get the endpoint. It is your collector at `https://<host>:4318/v1/traces`. The collector must be reachable from where your checks run. For public locations that means a public address with TLS in front. For private locations, an address reachable from the agent's network is enough.
2. Get the key. It depends on your setup.
   - Self-hosted ClickStack: in the HyperDX UI, open **Team Settings** > **API Keys** and copy the ingestion key.
   - Managed ClickStack in ClickHouse Cloud: there is no hosted OTLP endpoint. Run the ClickStack collector pointed at ClickHouse Cloud, and use the `OTLP_AUTH_TOKEN` you set on it as the key.
3. Paste the values into Checkly. On the [Traces settings page](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/account/traces), turn on **Enable exporting traces**, paste the URL, and add the header from the table below. Use the bare key, without a `Bearer` prefix.
4. Find the spans. In ClickStack, the spans appear under service `checkly`.

| Header name | Value |
|---|---|
| `authorization` | `<ingestion key or OTLP_AUTH_TOKEN>` |

ClickStack documents the collector for [self-hosted ClickStack](https://clickhouse.com/docs/use-cases/observability/clickstack/ingesting-data/opentelemetry) and for [Managed ClickStack](https://clickhouse.com/docs/clickstack/managed-onboarding/setting-up-your-opentelemetry-collector).
