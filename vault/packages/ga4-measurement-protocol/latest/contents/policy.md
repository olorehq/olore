# Policy

## Authorization

You must have full rights to use the Measurement Protocol, including necessary authorizations from rights holders and account owners.

## User Notice & Consent

You must:
- Inform end users about your Google Analytics implementation and data collection
- Obtain consent from end users, or provide them with the opportunity to opt out

## Prohibited Data

Do **not** send:
- Personally identifiable information (names, SSNs, email addresses)
- Permanent device identifiers that cannot be reset

## Session Stitching

You must **not** stitch authenticated and unauthenticated sessions unless:
- End users have consented to such activity
- Such merging is allowed under applicable laws and regulations

## Advertising Features

If using advertising-related analytics tools, comply with:
- Google Analytics Advertising Features Policy
- Google Play Developer Program Policies

## Enforcement

Policy violations can result in **account termination** and **permanent loss** of analytics data.
